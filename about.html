About Me

My Background

Welcome to Meresight Video Production, a portfolio showcasing my work in film, video, and visual culture.

With a Film and History of Art and Architecture background from Trinity College Dublin and Boston College, and an Interactive Digital Media M.Sc from Trinity, my work explores moving images as a form of digital construction of culture and expression through many styles of visual storytelling. This portfolio brings together projects across short films, video essays, music videos, and documentary that I have explored over the years, to demonstrate my versatility and passion for visual engagement.

I am committed to creating compelling narratives that resonate with audiences, and I am always seeking new opportunities to collaborate and bring emotions to life through the lens of a camera.
